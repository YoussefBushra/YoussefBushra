I'm a **Backend-Focused Full-Stack Software Engineer** who enjoys turning complex business requirements into reliable, scalable software.

My experience has centered around building and evolving **production SaaS platforms with large distributed architectures**, working across backend services, data, integrations, business workflows, and user-facing applications.

I'm particularly interested in **backend architecture, distributed systems, system design, performance, and building products end-to-end**.

## 🧠 Engineering Focus

### 🏗️ Distributed Systems

Designing and developing systems composed of multiple independently deployed services, with clear boundaries between business domains and reliable service-to-service communication.

### 🔄 Event-Driven Architecture

Working with asynchronous workflows, message brokers, events, request/response messaging, and background processing to decouple services and support complex business flows.

### 🏢 Multi-Tenant SaaS

Building systems where tenant isolation, authorization, configuration, and business rules are fundamental parts of the architecture rather than afterthoughts.

### 📊 Data & Performance

Working with relational and document databases, caching, search infrastructure, data-intensive workloads, and performance optimization.

### 🔭 Observability

Building systems that can be understood in production through structured logging, distributed tracing, metrics, and centralized observability.

### 🔌 Enterprise Integrations

Designing integrations with external systems and APIs while handling data mapping, synchronization, reliability, failures, and complex business workflows.
